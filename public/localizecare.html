What the service does

LocalizeCare is a human-reviewed monitoring service for agencies that maintain multilingual websites. After site updates, I check public pages for quiet localization regressions:

  • Page-language metadata that doesn't match the content
  • Alternate-language relationships that aren't reciprocal
  • Language switchers that land on the wrong page
  • Customer-facing controls falling back to the wrong language

Findings arrive as a short weekly exception report. Each issue includes the URL, locale, expected and actual behavior, reproduction steps, evidence, and a suggested owner, and previously reported issues are rechecked for open or resolved status.

Checks cover public pages only — no credentials, no installations, no site changes.
